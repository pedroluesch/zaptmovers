What makes a Dallas move different

Four things here cost people time and money when the moving company has not worked the city before.

High-rises run on a schedule, not on yours

Uptown, Victory Park and Turtle Creek buildings assign move windows, require a reserved freight elevator and want a certificate of insurance days ahead. Many will not allow moves on weekends at all. We book that window with your building and plan the crew around it — the building's calendar decides your move date more than your own does.

In the M Streets and Lakewood, the alley is the front door

Tudor and Craftsman homes east of Central have no driveway to speak of and load from a narrow alley with power lines overhead. We send a truck sized for the alley and carry the distance rather than blocking a neighbor in.

Historic districts limit where a truck can sit

Swiss Avenue, Munger Place and Junius Heights have parking rules and protected trees that decide where we can stage. We scout it beforehand so nobody gets a ticket and no branch gets clipped.

Central and the tollway set the clock

A crew crossing 75 or the Dallas North Tollway in rush hour burns time you pay for. We start early or run mid-morning, and we say which window is realistic instead of promising one we cannot hold.

Dallas questions we get every week

Can you handle my building's certificate of insurance and elevator reservation?

Yes, and there is no charge. Send us your building management contact when you book. We carry general liability, workers' compensation and up to $1 million in cargo coverage, which meets the requirements we have seen across Dallas high-rises.

My building only allows moves on weekdays. Is that a problem?

No. Most Dallas high-rises restrict move windows to business hours and we schedule around them. Book early, because those windows are limited and buildings hand them out first come.

Can your truck get down my alley?

Usually, with the right truck. We ask about alley width, overhead lines and gate placement before quoting, and send a smaller truck where it is needed.

Do you move offices in downtown Dallas?

Yes, on evenings and weekends so your team logs in Monday like nothing happened. We coordinate dock access with building management.

How far ahead should I book?

Three to four weeks for a high-rise, because the building's elevator calendar is the real constraint. Two weeks is usually fine for a house.
