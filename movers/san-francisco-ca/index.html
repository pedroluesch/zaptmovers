What makes a San Francisco move different

Four things here cost people time and money when the moving company has not worked the city before.

The parking permit has to be requested days ahead

Without a posted temporary no-parking permit, there may be nowhere legal for the truck to stop on your block, and the carry gets long and expensive. The city needs advance notice and the signs have to go up before move day. We handle the request as part of the job — this is the number one thing people do not know to plan for here.

Victorian and Edwardian stairs decide the plan

Narrow entryways, tight landings and switchback staircases mean some furniture will not go up the way it came down. We look at the difficult pieces during the walkthrough and decide then whether they come apart or go through a window, instead of finding out with your sofa on the sidewalk.

Steep and narrow streets limit truck size

Parts of the city simply cannot take a full-size truck. We send a smaller one and shuttle where we have to. A company that shows up with the wrong truck turns a five-hour job into a nine-hour one.

SoMa and Mission Bay buildings run on reservations

High-rises assign move windows, require a reserved freight elevator and want a certificate of insurance ahead of time, often weekdays only. We book that with your building before the date is confirmed.

San Francisco questions we get every week

Do I need a parking permit for the moving truck?

On most blocks, yes. The city issues a temporary no-parking permit and the signs must be posted in advance. We take care of the request when you book — leave enough lead time and it is a non-issue.

I live in a third-floor walk-up. Does that cost more?

It affects the quote, because stairs take time. We ask how many flights and whether the staircase turns, then price it honestly up front instead of adding a stair fee on move day.

Will your truck fit on my street?

We check before quoting. Where the street is too narrow or too steep, we bring a smaller truck and shuttle. It is planned, not improvised.

Can you do my building's certificate of insurance and elevator booking?

Yes, at no charge. Send us the building management contact when you book.

Do you move from San Francisco to Texas?

Regularly. We operate our own crews in Dallas-Fort Worth as well, so the same company loads and unloads your shipment.
