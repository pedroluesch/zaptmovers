What makes a Arlington move different

Four things here cost people time and money when the moving company has not worked the city before.

We check the stadium calendar before your date

AT&T Stadium, Globe Life Field and Six Flags can shut down access across a wide stretch of the city, and a truck stuck in event traffic costs you hours. We look at the schedule before we confirm a date and tell you if the day you picked is a bad idea. Almost nobody does this, and it is the single most useful local thing we do here.

Entertainment District addresses need earlier starts

If you live anywhere near the venues, the safe move is a very early crew or a mid-week date. We will say so rather than take the booking and hope.

UTA turnover fills August and May

Student housing and apartment complexes around the university cycle hard twice a year, with assigned move-in windows. Those weeks book out first.

A mixed housing stock needs different trucks

Arlington runs from 1960s single-story ranch homes with carports to new apartment complexes with elevators and loading zones. We match the truck and crew to the address instead of sending the same setup everywhere.

Arlington questions we get every week

Is a game day a bad time to move?

Usually yes, if you are anywhere near the Entertainment District. We check the stadium schedule before confirming your date and will suggest a different one if traffic is going to cost you money.

Do you move students near UTA?

Yes, and we know those complexes assign move windows. August and May book out early, so give us as much notice as you can.

Can you move me between Arlington and Fort Worth or Dallas?

Constantly. It is one of the most common moves we run, and it stays a local move on the quote.

Do you handle apartment complexes with elevators?

Yes. We reserve the elevator with management and send the certificate of insurance they require, at no charge.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to four weeks, and further out for August, May and the end of any month.
